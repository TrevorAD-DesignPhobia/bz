Play with friends online. Create a room, send the link, and play from your own devices.

How to play
  1. Take every tile of one colour from a factory; the rest slide to the centre. Or take one colour from the centre (the first to do so takes the first-player marker and a floor penalty).
  2. Place them in one pattern line. A line holds one colour, and not a colour already on that wall row. Extra tiles fall to the floor.
  3. When all tiles are taken, each full line sends one tile to the wall and scores for adjacent tiles. Floor tiles cost 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3.
  4. The game ends after the round in which someone completes a wall row. Bonuses: row +2, column +7, all five of a colour +10.
